# Product Launch Plan - Theme TwentyOne

## Modern Tailwind + Vite Theme

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Launch Date:** September 15, 2026 (Target - v1.0)  
**Launch Type:** Major Release (Beta to v1.0)  
**Owner:** Theme Product Team

---

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the launch plan for Theme TwentyOne v1.0, marking the transition from beta to production-ready status. The launch positions TwentyOne as the developer's choice for modern Laravel applications, emphasizing speed, developer experience, and production-ready components.

### Launch Objectives

1. **Awareness:** Achieve 60% awareness among target developer segment
2. **Adoption:** Secure 50 new projects within 90 days of launch
3. **Engagement:** Grow community to 200+ active members
4. **Validation:** Obtain 5+ public case studies from early adopters

## Risk Management

### Launch Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Critical bug discovered | Medium | High | Rollback plan, hotfix team |
| Low initial adoption | Medium | Medium | Paid promotion boost |
| Negative feedback | Low | High | Rapid response, transparency |
| Infrastructure issues | Low | High | Redundancy, monitoring |

### Contingency Plans

#### Critical Bug at Launch
1. Acknowledge within 1 hour
2. Deploy rollback if critical
3. Communicate fix timeline
4. Deploy hotfix within 24h
5. Follow-up communication

#### Low Webinar Registration
1. Activate partner network
2. Increase social frequency
3. Ensure recording available
